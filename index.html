STARTEASY ZAMBIA

End-to-End Business Operations & Customer Journey

The objective is to ensure that StartEasy does not simply attract enquiries but systematically converts them into paying customers, delivers the promised services and generates repeat business.

Marketing → Lead Gen → Acquisition → Sales → Quotation → Payment → Delivery → Retention

1. The Complete Customer Lifecycle

Every customer moves through a clearly defined journey, with strict accountability and handover rules.

STAGE 01

Marketing

Marketing
Awareness • Campaigns • Website • Social media. Attracts the right audience to generate enquiries.
Handover: Marketing → Sales
STAGE 02

Lead Generation & Acquisition

Sales
Enquiries • Lead capture • Customer identification. Captures and organises potential customers into the CRM.
Handover: Sales → Sales
STAGE 03

Sales & Consultation

Sales
Needs assessment • Follow-up • Conversion. Understands needs and recommends the appropriate service.
Handover: Sales → Operations (for tech)
STAGE 04

Quotation & Approval

Sales + Finance
Pricing • Service scope • Acceptance. Formalises the commercial agreement before delivery.
Handover: Sales → Finance
STAGE 05

Payment & Onboarding

Finance
Invoicing • Payment confirmation • Job creation. Gatekeeper rule: Operations cannot start without payment.
Handover: Finance → Operations
STAGE 06

Service Delivery

Operations
Assignment • Execution • Quality assurance. Delivers what was paid for, tracked by a unique Service Job ID.
Handover: Operations → Customer Support
STAGE 07

Completion & Support

Operations
Handover • Feedback • Issue resolution. Ensures the customer receives and understands their deliverables.
Handover: Operations → Customer
STAGE 08

Retention & Referrals

Support / Sales
Repeat purchases • Relationships • Recommendations. Turns one-time customers into long-term clients.
Handover: Support → Marketing
